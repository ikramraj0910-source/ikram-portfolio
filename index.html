  1. Challenge

    Founders entering the UAE face a real choice of structures, each with its own licensing, approval and banking steps. Most want one advisor who explains the options plainly and keeps every step moving.

    My role

    Business Setup Advisor. I own the client relationship from first enquiry to a licensed, bank-ready company, and manage the pipeline in Zoho CRM.

    Approach

    1. Discovery Consultative sessions on activity, ownership and goals.
    2. Structure Mainland, Free Zone or Offshore, with an ownership model and cost projection.
    3. Licensing & approvals Permits and regulatory approvals with the authorities.
    4. PRO & visas Documentation with internal PRO and legal teams.
    5. Banking Corporate account opening with banking partners.

    Tools

    Zoho CRM · Pipeline reporting

    Outcome

    One point of contact for each client, with transparent procedures, timelines and regular status updates from lead to licence.
