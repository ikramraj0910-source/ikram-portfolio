About

Client-facing by training, operational by habit.

My career started on the phones. As a Sales Executive for a US company, I spent two years cold calling, writing outreach emails and prospecting on LinkedIn, and I learned to move a conversation from first contact to a closed deal.

From there I moved into service and operations. At Tridel's DelSuites and Delrentals, I looked after owners, tenants and guests across a Canadian rental portfolio, coordinating inspections, renewals, vendors and housekeeping so the experience felt seamless from the outside. At Bridgeway Firm International, I joined as a Scheduler and went on to lead the workforce scheduling function for a 24/7 healthcare staffing desk, working with senior leadership on staffing strategy and reporting.

Today I advise founders and companies on setting up in the UAE: choosing between Mainland, Free Zone and Offshore structures, then coordinating licensing, approvals, PRO services and business banking. Alongside this, I have supported Amazon and Noon sellers with listings, content, advertising and day-to-day marketplace operations.

Across every role, my approach has stayed the same. I take ownership of the outcome, keep everyone informed, and turn recurring work into clear processes. That interest in repeatable systems is what is drawing me toward Python and AI.

Advisory · SRTIP Accelerator · Current role

UAE business setup & company formation

Context

Founders and companies entering the UAE face a real choice of structures, each with its own licensing, approval and banking steps. Most want one advisor who explains the options plainly and keeps the whole process moving.

My role

Business Setup Advisor. I own the client relationship from the first enquiry to a licensed, bank-ready company, and I manage that pipeline in Zoho CRM.

Approach

  1. Discovery. Consultative sessions to understand the activity, ownership and goals.
  2. Structure. Recommend a Mainland, Free Zone or Offshore setup with an ownership model and cost projection.
  3. Licensing & approvals. Coordinate permits and regulatory approvals with the licensing authorities.
  4. PRO & visas. Work with internal PRO and legal teams on documentation.
  5. Banking. Liaise with banking partners on corporate account opening.

Tools

  • Zoho CRM
  • Client pipeline reporting

Outcome

Clients get a single point of contact, transparent procedures and timelines, and regular status updates as their file moves from lead to licence.
